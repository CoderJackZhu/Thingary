从拥有，到打算

看清现在，
再想下一步。

物品留下记录，盘点留下事实。
未来的计划，从自己的家底出发。

账户与盘点

分散的余额，
完整的家底。

银行卡、余额宝、券商、公积金，还有房贷，也可以记外币账户和按份额持有的投资。把资产与欠款放在一起，定期核对余额，看清净资产如何变化。

每月或每季盘点一次。
不必把生活变成一笔笔流水。

目标与规划

想过的生活，
有可以核对的计划。

需要存多少，什么时候能不靠工资，歇一阵能撑多久。用自己的余额、投入和假设，慢慢算清楚。

每月要存多少？

填入目标时间与生活开销，反算每月需要的投入；复用已有余额，不用再抄一张表。

什么时候能不靠工资？

查看财务独立时间的估算，按需展开依据与假设。条件没填齐会明确说明，不把未知当作零。

歇一阵能撑多久？

临时比较空窗、开销或换工作的影响。试算不改账户余额，结论也不是收益承诺。

用心拥有的，值得留个档。

照片、购买成本、维修与保障，放在同一份物品档案里。可以独立使用，也可以按需开启。

偶尔回头，看看走了多远。

近 1 年、今年以来、全部或自选日期，回顾积累与支出。净资产变化和投资收益，始终分开看。

From what you own to what comes next

See where you are.
Then plan ahead.

Keep a record of your belongings and balances.
Build your plans on your own starting point.

Account check-ins

Separate accounts.
One clear picture.

Bring balances and debts together, including bank accounts, investments, housing provident funds, and loans; foreign-currency accounts and share-based holdings are supported. Check in periodically to follow your net worth.

Once a month or quarter.
No daily transaction logging required.

Goals and planning

Your life plans.
Numbers you can check.

How much to save, when you could leave your paycheck, or how long you could take off. Explore each question with your own balances and assumptions.

How much should I save each month?

Estimate the monthly contribution needed for your target date and living costs, using the balances you already recorded.

When could I leave my paycheck?

Explore a possible date, then inspect its assumptions. Missing information stays visible; it is never treated as zero.

How long could I take off?

Compare a work break, different expenses, or a job change without changing account balances. Estimates are not promised returns.

A place for the things you keep.

Photos, purchase costs, repairs, and warranties, together. Use belongings records independently, or enable them when you need them.

Look back. See what has changed.

Review the last year, year to date, all time, or custom dates. Net-worth changes and investment profit stay distinct.

自己的资料，自己保管

生活的底稿，
留在自己的设备里。

无需注册，也不用上传家底。
没有行情联网或 AI，日常使用完全离线；想在多台设备间使用同一份资料，可以自己开启同步，连接你自己的 WebDAV（如坚果云、Nextcloud），内容加密后才上传，默认关闭。电脑版点击「检查更新」，或主动开启「启动时检查更新」后，才查询公开发布列表；下载与安装由你手动完成。即将推出的 iPhone 版同样默认不联网，更新由 App Store 管理；它与电脑版默认各自独立保存资料，也可以用同一套可选同步，或靠备份文件搬运。

本地记录，备份由你掌握

主资料库和图片未加密；完整备份可以使用你保管的恢复密钥加密。自动备份与资料同盘，请另留一份异地备份。

适合盘点，不替你做决定

金额以人民币记录，账户类型面向中国大陆。房产不计入金融净资产。不提供日常流水、交易或自动付款，规划估算不构成收益承诺。

网页也保持简单

这张页面不加载第三方脚本、网络字体或统计服务。所有应用截图使用虚构样例资料。

Your records, in your hands

A picture of your life.
Kept on your own devices.

No registration or uploading your balances. No market feed or AI. Normal use works offline; if you want the same library on several devices you can switch on optional sync to your own WebDAV (Jianguoyun, Nextcloud…), encrypted before upload and off by default. On desktop, public release lists are queried when you click Check for updates, or at most once per 24 hours on startup if you enable startup checks. Downloads and installation remain manual. The upcoming iPhone version also stays offline by default and is updated through the App Store. It keeps its own library unless you turn on the same optional sync, or you can move data with backup files.

Local records. Backups you control.

The main database and photos are not encrypted. Full backups can be encrypted with a recovery key you keep. Automatic backups share the same disk as your data; keep an off-device copy too.

A clearer picture, not financial promises.

Amounts are recorded in CNY. Account types are designed for mainland China. Property is excluded from financial net worth. No daily transaction ledger, trading, or automatic payments. Estimates are not promised returns.

A simple website, too.

This page loads no third-party scripts, remote fonts, or analytics. All app screenshots use fictional records. The app currently has a Simplified Chinese interface only.
